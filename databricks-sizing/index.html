OPEN ASSUMPTIONS

An estimate you can explain.

Published rates × estimated usage. The rate is sourced; the usage is a planning model.

Batch & streaming

Batch DBUs = daily GB × 30.42 days × DBU/GB × headroom. Simple / standard / complex starts at 0.08 / 0.20 / 0.50 DBU/GB. The completion window sets required average DBU/h during processing; it does not change total estimated DBUs. Streaming uses the larger of throughput-derived DBU/h and the baseline floor, runs 730 hours, and applies a complexity factor. These editable throughput assumptions are not vendor benchmarks.

SQL & data science

SQL uses retained dataset TB and query profile, independently of daily ingestion. Dashboard / interactive / heavy assumes 0.1% / 1% / 10% scanned per query, a minimum 2X-Small / X-Small / Small warehouse and 20 / 10 / 5 users per cluster. The size index increases with ceil(log₂(max(1, scanned GB / 100))), subject to that minimum. Monthly DBUs = size DBU/h × clusters × scheduled hours × active percentage × headroom. These are planning heuristics; validate scans, spills, queues and latency with a query profile. CPU notebooks use people × active hours × days × DBU/h × headroom. GPU training is excluded.

GenAI & retrieval

Model cost = requests × input/output tokens × the respective USD per million token rate. Answer profiles fill token assumptions; advanced controls allow custom tokens and model choice. AI Search uses one standard unit per 2 million 768-dimensional vectors, billed for 730 hours. Vector bytes use float32; index metadata is excluded. Embeddings, sync pipelines, reranking, agent/tool calls and retries need separate allowances.

Lakebase & storage

Lakebase reuses the shared Lakebase calculator limits: 2 GB RAM/CU, 0.5 CU then integers, autoscaling to 64 CU with at most a 16 CU range, and fixed capacity to 112 CU. Working set × headroom determines a capacity target held while active, not predicted average autoscaling use. Excess capacity requirements or unsupported regions make the estimate incomplete. Above 32 CU or with HA, compute is billed for 730 hours. Eligible scheduled use assumes scale to zero has been configured; idle-timeout consumption is excluded. HA adds one equal secondary. PITR and snapshots are excluded.

For SQL, storage = retained dataset TB × 1000 × layer/compression factor. Other lakehouse scenarios use processed GB/day × net-new share × retention × layer factor. Updates can consume compute without growing stored data.

Saved estimates

Share links preserve the applied unit rates for the selected deployment and cloud comparison, inputs, overrides, comparison geography and sizing version. Saved rates are supplied in the link and are labelled separately from freshly fetched official rates. Refresh explicitly recalculates using the official feed and snapshot fallback. Changing to a workload or region absent from the saved rates uses the bundled snapshot. Legacy input-only links recalculate with current assumptions. Incompatible sizing versions are rejected rather than silently changing a saved estimate.

Budget range, discounts & boundaries

The range applies an editable ±35% sensitivity to the complete monthly estimate; it is not a statistical confidence interval. Capacity headroom increases compute usage separately. Discount defaults to zero and applies only to eligible compute and AI Search endpoint lines, not tokens, storage or manual allowances. A 730-hour month is used for continuous workloads; business hours are 8 × 22, extended hours are 12 × 30.

Totals exclude taxes, networking/egress, private connectivity, support plans, implementation effort, classic VM instances, ingestion connectors, extra workspaces/environments, cross-region DR and unlisted services. Add an explicit monthly allowance where needed. Regional prices do not guarantee service availability or contractual terms. Azure rates are published by Databricks for reference; Microsoft sets Azure Databricks pricing. Azure Premium corresponds to AWS/GCP Enterprise for platform tier comparison.
