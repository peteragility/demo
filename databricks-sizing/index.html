OPEN ASSUMPTIONS

An estimate you can explain.

Published rates × estimated usage. The rate is sourced; the usage is a planning model.

Batch & streaming

Batch DBUs = daily GB × 30.42 days × DBU/GB × headroom. Simple / standard / complex starts at 0.08 / 0.20 / 0.50 DBU/GB. Streaming uses the larger of throughput-derived DBU/h and the baseline floor, runs 730 hours, and applies a complexity factor. These throughput assumptions are editable and are not vendor benchmarks.

SQL & data science

SQL selects a starter warehouse from daily volume and transformation complexity. Monthly DBUs = warehouse DBU/h × assumed clusters × scheduled hours × active percentage × headroom. One cluster per 20 concurrent users is a heuristic, not a concurrency guarantee. CPU notebooks use people × active hours × days × DBU/h × headroom. GPU training is excluded.

GenAI & retrieval

Model cost = requests × input/output tokens × the respective USD per million token rate. AI Search uses one standard unit per 2 million 768-dimensional vectors, billed for 730 hours. Vector bytes use float32; index metadata is excluded. Embeddings, sync pipelines, reranking, agent/tool calls and retries need separate allowances.

Lakebase & storage

Lakebase assumes 2 GB RAM per autoscaling CU. Working set × headroom determines an estimated average CU allocation, rounded up in half-CU steps. It uses on-demand rates. Optional HA adds one equal-size secondary and bills both computes for 730 hours. Database storage is separate; PITR and snapshots are excluded. Lakehouse storage is steady-state daily GB × retention × retained-size factor, not month-one growth.

Budget range, discounts & boundaries

The range applies an editable ±35% sensitivity to the complete monthly estimate; it is not a statistical confidence interval. Capacity headroom increases compute usage separately. Discount defaults to zero and applies only to eligible compute and AI Search endpoint lines, not tokens, storage or manual allowances. A 730-hour month is used for continuous workloads; business hours are 8 × 22, extended hours are 12 × 30.

Totals exclude taxes, networking/egress, private connectivity, support plans, implementation effort, classic VM instances, ingestion connectors, extra workspaces/environments, cross-region DR and unlisted services. Add an explicit monthly allowance where needed. Regional prices do not guarantee service availability or contractual terms. Azure rates are published by Databricks for reference; Microsoft sets Azure Databricks pricing. Azure Premium corresponds to AWS/GCP Enterprise for platform tier comparison.
