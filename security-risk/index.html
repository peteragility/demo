Scope & dates

High risk means published CVSS ≥ 7.0, confirmed exploitation in CISA KEV, an automated source-text signal of shared-trust/distribution compromise, or a reviewed urgent notice. The default Impact + CVSS ranking applies the same rules to every issue: (1) source text reporting compromise of shared signing/certificate-authority keys or software distribution; (2) KEV-confirmed exploitation with CVSS ≥ 9.0 or known ransomware use; (3) CVSS ≥ 9.0 with a low-complexity network attack, no privileges or user interaction, and no additional CVSS v4 prerequisite; (4) all others by CVSS. Within groups: CVSS, KEV evidence, EPSS, then risk date. Choose CVSS highest to sort by severity alone.

No manual ranking boosts. Product names, CVE IDs, categories and editorial urgency flags do not determine priority. The shared-trust/distribution signal is a conservative automated inference from source text, not a verified count of affected servers, organizations or people. Missing metrics stay unknown. Expand any row for its rule and source evidence.

Last 30 days includes today and the preceding 29 days in Hong Kong time. The archive contains older entries within six calendar months. Risk dates reflect disclosure, a new KEV alert, or a confirmed material advisory update. Routine source checks do not renew a risk's date.

CVSS is severity out of 10; — means unpublished or not applicable. KEV confirms exploitation. “Not in KEV” does not establish safety. Details show affected versions, prerequisites, fixes and official sources.

A focused information brief from monitored sources, not a complete inventory of every vulnerability. Confirm vendor guidance and distribution backports for your environment.

Sources & refresh health

GitHub Actions checks sources, reviews eligibility and dates, and publishes the current 30-day list and six-month archive nightly at 23:17 Asia/Hong_Kong. GitHub may delay scheduled starts. Failed sources retain prior data with its original dates.
