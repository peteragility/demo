Scope & dates

High risk means published CVSS ≥ 7.0, confirmed exploitation in CISA KEV, or a reviewed urgent incident such as a CA compromise. CVSS scores rank highest first; unscored entries remain visible at the end.

Last 30 days includes today and the preceding 29 days in Hong Kong time. The archive contains older entries within six calendar months. Risk dates reflect disclosure, a new KEV alert, or a confirmed material advisory update. Routine source checks do not renew a risk's date.

CVSS is severity out of 10; — means unpublished or not applicable. KEV confirms exploitation. “Not in KEV” does not establish safety. Details show affected versions, prerequisites, fixes and official sources.

A focused information brief from monitored sources, not a complete inventory of every vulnerability. Confirm vendor guidance and distribution backports for your environment.

Sources & refresh health

GitHub Actions checks sources, reviews eligibility and dates, and publishes the current 30-day list and six-month archive nightly at 23:17 Asia/Hong_Kong. GitHub may delay scheduled starts. Failed sources retain prior data with its original dates.
