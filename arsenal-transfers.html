🧠 Yuki's Transfer Analysis

🔴 STRIKER — Critical Gap CRITICAL

Arsenal's biggest weakness. Havertz is not a natural #9, and Jesus is injury-prone and potentially leaving. Must sign a world-class striker. Alvarez is the dream but unlikely. Gyökeres is the pragmatic option — proven goal machine, knows the league's style. If both fail, Arsenal risk going into the season under-equipped up top. Title defence demands a 20+ goal striker.

🟠 MIDFIELD — Elite Upgrade Needed HIGH

Rice + Ødegaard is world-class, but the #8/#10 rotation needs strengthening. Nørgaard is solid but not elite — Arteta doesn't fully trust him in big games. Rogers is the ideal signing: young, versatile, Premier League proven, can play multiple positions. Guimarães would be transformative — elite ball progression, physicality, and creativity. If only one big signing happens, midfield or striker should be it.

🟡 DEFENCE — Saliba Uncertainty HIGH

Saliba's injury is a massive concern. Surgery option still on the table — could be out for months. Hincapié is a smart signing (versatile, young, ball-playing). But if Saliba is out long-term, Arsenal need another CB. Gabriel + Hincapié + Timber is okay, but Champions League depth requires more. Monitor Saliba situation closely — if surgery, must sign another CB.

🟢 WINGER — Well Covered MEDIUM

Saka + Tzolis + Martinelli + Nwaneri. Tzolis replaces Trossard with younger, faster profile. Nwaneri's emergence makes this position deep. Barcola would be luxury — exciting but not essential. Focus budget on striker + midfield first.

🔵 GOALKEEPER — Sorted LOW

Raya #1, Kepa #2, Meslier #3. Solid depth. No action needed.

📋 SUMMARY: What Arsenal Should Do

Must-do: 1) Sign a striker (Gyökeres or bust). 2) Sign Rogers or Guimarães for midfield.
Should-do: 3) Monitor Saliba — prepare CB backup plan. 4) Sell Jesus if good offer arrives.
Nice-to-have: 5) Barcola as luxury winger signing if budget allows.

Predicted spending: ~£200-250M net. Champions PL prize money + CL revenue covers it. Berta knows this window defines the title defence. COYG! 🔴⚽
