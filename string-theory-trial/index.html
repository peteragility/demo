The Trial Begins

Six decades of elegance, evasion, and elusive evidence

1968
Origin

The Euler-Beta Formula

Gabriele Veneziano plucks an 18th-century equation from mathematical obscurity to describe particle collisions. This becomes the founding discovery of string theory — though nobody knows it yet.

1969
Defense

Strings, Not Points

Susskind, Nambu & Nielsen independently realize: particles aren't infinitesimal dots, but vibrating strings. Different frequencies = different particles. Reviewers dismiss it. "I felt like I'd gotten hit over the head with a trash can." — Susskind

1973
Prosecution

The QCD Assassination

Stanford's accelerator confirms quarks inside protons. Quantum Chromodynamics perfectly explains the strong force. Original string theory is dead. Researchers flee the field.

1974
Defense

The Graviton Emerges

Closed strings naturally produce a massless spin-2 particle — the exact signature of the graviton. Without even trying, string theory conjures gravity from mathematics. The dream of a Theory of Everything is reborn.

1971
Witness

26 Dimensions & Supersymmetry

Claude Lovelace (no PhD, physics professor, parakeet owner) discovers stability at 26 dimensions. Yuri Golfand proposes supersymmetry — every boson has a fermion partner (squarks, selectrons, gluinos). Dimensions drop to 10. Tachyons vanish.

1985
Defense

First Superstring Revolution

Calabi & Yau provide the origami: manifolds that fold 6 extra dimensions invisible while preserving supersymmetry. Green & Schwartz fix致命 anomalies. But there isn't one theory — there are five.

1995
Defense

M-Theory — The Unification

Edward Witten — history → economics → philosophy → physics → Fields Medal — reveals that all 5 theories are shadows of one 11-dimensional framework. Branes emerge as the real building blocks. Strings are just one type.

1995
Defense

D-Branes Are Real

Joseph Polchinski proves D-branes aren't mathematical fictions — they have mass, carry charge, and warp spacetime. "If there's one thing I hope I'm remembered for..." He later lost his battle with brain cancer, 2018.

1997
Witness

The Holographic Universe

Juan Maldacena publishes the most-cited paper in string theory history. ADS/CFT: a universe with gravity = a flat boundary without gravity. Reality might be a hologram. But our universe isn't Anti-de Sitter...

1998
Prosecution

Dark Energy Bombshell

Perlmutter announces the universe's expansion is accelerating. String theory naturally produces negative curvature. Our universe has positive curvature. The theory points the wrong way.

2003
Prosecution

The Landscape & The Swampland

KKLT mechanism needs 10⁵⁰⁰ possible universes to fix dark energy. Kamran Vafa then shows most of them are in the "Swampland" — incompatible with string theory's own rules. Dark energy AND inflation may be forbidden.

2025
Prosecution

The Missing Superpartners

A decade of searches — IceCube (Antarctic ice), LUX-ZEPLIN (abandoned gold mine), LHC (Geneva) — zero supersymmetric particles. The foundation string theory rests on remains experimentally invisible.

Closing Arguments

Six decades of evidence, weighed

🛡️ For the Defense

  • Only framework that unifies all 4 forces, including gravity
  • Closed strings naturally produce the graviton (spin-2)
  • ADS/CFT is now used in condensed matter, nuclear physics & quantum information
  • D-branes are real physical objects with mass and charge
  • Black hole information paradox resolved via holographic principle
  • Quarks, black holes & parity violation were also once dismissed
  • "Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence"

🔪 For the Prosecution

  • Zero experimental confirmation in ~60 years
  • No supersymmetric particles found by LHC, IceCube, or LUX-ZEPLIN
  • Requires 7 extra dimensions that have never been observed
  • 10⁵⁰⁰ possible universes = no predictive power
  • ADS/CFT describes a universe unlike our own
  • Swampland rules out most dark energy & inflation solutions
  • Every experimental failure → theory shape-shifts again
MISTRIAL

Beauty has been both savior and saboteur. It rescued string theory when evidence failed, but also lured it into existential cul-de-sacs it could not escape. The file remains open — pending testimony only the universe can provide.
