Batch, Flex, Priority and off-peak prices are in the row details and are not compared; DeepSeek and Alibaba time-of-day prices use peak / busy-hour rates. Models with no comparable hyperscaler price are left out of the parity count, as are models where a hyperscaler sells an unconfirmed checkpoint for less. Tap a row for one card per platform: every endpoint and tier with input, output and cache prices, where it runs (in-region, geo or global; Hong Kong and Taiwan), notes, IDs and sources.

Regions come from each platform's official region tables; Databricks regions refresh daily. In-region = processed in the region you call; Geo = stays in one geography (US, EU, a data zone); Global / Cross-geo = may be processed anywhere.
