Each cell is input / output USD per 1M text tokens: the platform's cheapest standard (real-time, on-demand) price for that model version, in any region or processing scope. Databricks = published DBU rate × $0.07. Δ compares it with Databricks' cheapest standard price at the blend above. Batch, Flex, Priority and off-peak prices are in the row details and are not compared; DeepSeek and Alibaba time-of-day prices use peak / busy-hour rates. Models with no comparable hyperscaler price are left out of the parity count, as are models where a hyperscaler sells an unconfirmed checkpoint for less. Tap any row to expand it — one card per platform: every endpoint and tier with input, output and cache prices, where it runs (in-region, geo or global; Hong Kong and Taiwan), notes, IDs and sources.

Regions come from each platform's official region tables; Databricks regions refresh daily. In-region = processed in the region you call; Geo = stays in one geography (US, EU, a data zone); Global / Cross-geo = may be processed anywhere.

Prices move fast. Every official source is re-checked daily: unchanged offers show today's verified date, and a changed source marks its card “source changed · re-check” until it is reviewed. Prices change often: confirm on the provider's site before you buy. Promotion changes and retirements apply on your local date; unverified prices remain labelled ?.
