Each offer states its tier. Regional and Data Zone offers, unconfirmed snapshots, and unverified prices are excluded from global price deltas. Availability gaps are counted separately from price parity.

Databricks prices scale from the published DBU rates using your USD / DBU value (default $0.07). Input and output rates are rounded to the reviewed list prices.

The date applies verified scheduled promotion changes and Databricks retirements. It is an estimate using dated sources, not a prediction of unannounced price changes.

Text-token charges include billable reasoning output. Audio, generated images, tools, tax and dedicated GPU capacity need separate quotes. Cache eligibility and minimum cache sizes depend on the provider.
